# CareFlow AI 🏥🤖
### AI-Powered Healthcare Access & Clinic Workflow Assistant

**Better Access. Smarter Clinics. Faster Workflows.**

CareFlow AI is an AI-powered healthcare administration platform designed to improve patient accessibility and optimize clinic workflows. It helps patients navigate administrative processes while enabling clinic staff to organize requests, track progress, and identify operational bottlenecks.

Built for the **AI/ML in Healthcare — Healthcare Accessibility & Workflow Optimization** hackathon challenge.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Patients often face difficulties understanding healthcare administrative procedures, preparing required documents, and navigating appointment-related services. Meanwhile, clinic staff spend valuable time managing repetitive enquiries, incomplete requests, and administrative backlogs.

These challenges can lead to confusion, delays, and inefficient workflows.

## 💡 Our Solution

CareFlow AI brings patient assistance and clinic operations together in one platform.

It aims to:
- Simplify healthcare administrative information.
- Organize and categorize patient requests.
- Improve communication between patients and clinic staff.
- Track request status and identify unresolved tasks.
- Provide data-driven insights into administrative workflows.
- Make healthcare services easier to navigate through accessible interfaces.

## ✨ Key Features

### 🧠 AI Intelligence
- Smart categorization and summarization of administrative requests.
- Plain-language explanations of complex instructions.
- Missing-information identification.
- AI-assisted administrative recommendations.
- Rule-based fallback when an external AI service is unavailable.

### 👤 Patient Portal
- Submit administrative requests.
- Select request categories and accessibility preferences.
- Receive a unique request reference ID.
- Check request status.

### 🏥 Clinic Operations
- Manage incoming patient requests.
- Search and filter requests.
- Update request statuses.
- Record administrative notes.
- Identify overdue or incomplete requests.

### 📊 Analytics Dashboard
- Overview of total requests and request statuses.
- Request distribution by category.
- Administrative workload insights.
- Processing-time metrics when sufficient valid data exists.
- Interactive charts and operational summaries.

### ♿ Accessibility
- Clear, simple-language guidance.
- Language preferences.
- Support for administrative accessibility requirements.
- A user-friendly interface for patients and clinic staff.

- Clear distinction between suggestions and verified information.
